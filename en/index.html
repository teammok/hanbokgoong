Philosophy

What you see is
what you wear.

Hanbok Goong is judged on cloth and design alone. The garment you try on in the atelier is the garment you receive — the same silk, the same embroidery. Underskirt, shoes, bag and norigae are included, with no separate charge.

  • 01We do not compete on a low price.
  • 02We do not substitute cheaper cloth.
  • 03We do not charge extra for accessories.
  • 04We are judged only on fabric and design.

Every garment is prepared in our own separate workshop and is repaired, cleaned and sanitised in-house before and after each wearing.

Three Pillars

What we do best

A garment made for the stage and a garment made for the most important day of a life are not different things. We carry the form and colour built on the runway directly to our clients.

Designer

Hanbok Designer
Lee Eun Jin

Working principally in royal court hanbok, bridal hanbok and the contemporary wonsam, she reinterprets the sculptural beauty of traditional Korean dress for the present day. Through runways and cultural exchange abroad, she has spent a decade arguing for hanbok as living, wearable art.

“I design the tradition and the dignity that belong to hanbok. Only when it comes alive again on the stage of our own time does it become the most Korean kind of beauty.”
