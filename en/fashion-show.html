Runway & Ceremony

Fashion Show · Royal Court
Traditional & Bridal

This is where Hanbok Goong began and where it still centres. The form and colour of pieces shown at Vancouver Fashion Week, the Kennedy Hall in Washington D.C. and a VVIP show in Dubai are carried directly into wedding and ceremonial dress.

◆

Tested on the runway

A runway is the harshest test there is. A silhouette that holds through movement, and colour that survives stage lighting — we bring both across unchanged.

⚖

Formal rules, kept

The structure of wonsam, hwarot and dangui and the placement of gold-leaf motifs follow their own law. We rebuild within it, for today's body and today's venues.

✦

Our own workshop

Concept, pattern, sewing and fitting all happen in-house, which is how we can take on stage costume and bespoke commissions directly.

01 Fashion Show

On runways around the world

From the invited collection at Vancouver Fashion Week (VFW 2025 S/S) to stages in the United States, Brazil, Germany, France, India, Vietnam, the UAE and Thailand — eleven countries in all.

Vancouver Fashion Week
VFW 2025 S/S

Black organza layered with gold-leaf bands into a tiered silhouette; a grey line carrying ink-wash painting; and a line in deep red. The jeogori-and-chima structure is kept intact while volume and layering are rebuilt to runway scale.

  • Black & gold tiered line — the collection signature
  • Ink-wash (sumuk) print line — men's and women's
  • Red line — the colour of traditional wedding dress, in a modern cut
  • Menswear line — durumagi and trousers reconstructed

Where cultures meet,
hanbok is worn

02 Royal Court

Royal Court & Contemporary Wonsam

Dangui, wonsam and hwarot, rebuilt for today's proportions without breaking their formality. Where the gold leaf sits and how colour ranks — tradition sets the rules, and we look for a modern eye inside them.

Dress with rank

A yellow dangui over a gold-leaf seuran skirt; a black dangui set against green; a ceremonial robe carrying a blue rank badge. Our court hanbok is worn for editorial shoots, cultural events and the pyebaek ceremony alike.

  • Dangui · Wonsam · Hwarot · Seuran and daeran skirts
  • Gold leaf and embroidery placed by traditional rule
  • Widely used for palace shoots, cultural events and broadcast
  • Contemporary wonsam — the wonsam reworked as modern formalwear
03 Traditional Wedding

Hwarot and Wonsam

Worn once in a lifetime. We prepare the full set — the embroidered hwarot, the hansam sleeves, the jokduri crown and the doturak daenggi ribbon.

04 Bridal Hanbok

At the centre of bridal hanbok

For the ceremony, the photo shoot, the family meeting and the pyebaek. Contemporary in feeling, without losing the manners the garment carries.

The bride

Ivory and gold thread as a base, with layered organza building the volume. Designed to hold its scale and lustre even standing beside a western gown — which is why it is chosen so often for bridal editorials.

  • Ceremony hanbok · photo-shoot hanbok · pyebaek hanbok
  • Ivory, gold-thread and pastel bridal lines
  • Matching groom's hanbok available
  • Presented at the 2021 Bridal Hanbok Trend Show
