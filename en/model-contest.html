01 Hanbok Model Contest

Where hanbok models go

We compete on design, not on price. Every season brings a new palette and a new line, and models travel from across the country for it — more than a thousand of them so far. The winners they become are the proof.

A hanbok that knows
the competition

Stage lighting, the distance to the judges' table, the way a skirt travels through a walk — contest hanbok is engineered differently from everyday hanbok. Years at the side of these stages have taught us which colours survive the lights and which silhouettes read large from the back of the room.

  • 2026 Hanbok Model Contest in Korea — Grand Prize, Silver and Excellence winners
  • 2024 Korea Hanbok Model Contest — Mi (美) title winner in the final
  • 2023 Korea Hanbok Model Contest — all three titles
  • 2022 Korea Hanbok Model Contest — Seon (善) title
  • 2021 Seon (善) · 2020 Jin (眞) title winners' atelier
02 International Pageant

National Costume

In the national costume round, one garment has to stand for a whole country. We have dressed contestants representing Korea on international stages.

One garment,
one country

A vast blue-gradient silhouette; layered tiers of yellow organza. On an international stage the audience has seconds to read “Korea”, so we keep the traditional structure and push the scale of the silhouette until the garment is unmistakably hanbok from any distance.

  • National costume and stage hanbok, made to measure
  • Oversized silhouettes and colour engineered for stage lighting
  • 2022 Paris, France — Jin & Mi title winners' hanbok
  • 2022 Bangkok, Thailand — Jin (眞) title winner's hanbok
