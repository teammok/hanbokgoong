Mother of the Bride

Couture Hanbok
for the Mothers

In Korea the mothers of the bride and groom wear hanbok to the wedding, and the choice is not a small one. Mothers compare prices elsewhere, look around — and come back to us.

◆

High-grade cloth only

We do not substitute cheaper fabric. The lustre of the silk and the weight of the organza in your hand will tell you.

✦

The complete set

Underskirt, shoes, bag and a norigae or hair ornament are all included. Nothing is charged separately as an accessory.

⚖

Both mothers, matched

We choose the two garments together, so that the bride's and the groom's mother look their best standing side by side.

★

Cleaned in-house

Every garment is repaired, cleaned and sanitised in our own separate workshop. Return it exactly as it is.

2025 Collection

The 2025 collection

Photographed fresh each year to capture that season's colour and line. Everything shown here is available to wear.

Depth of colour
creates dignity

Jaju (deep magenta), navy, wine, ink black — the mother's place calls for colours with low chroma and real depth. We choose fabric one tone deeper than looks right in daylight, so that it does not wash out under strong reception lighting.

  • Jaju · navy · wine · ink black — the classical line
  • Colours proven under wedding-hall lighting
  • The formal white jeogori with a coloured chima

Light, but
never slight

More mothers now ask for soft, luminous colours — grey-lilac, sage, apricot, blue-grey. The lighter the colour, the more nakedly the quality of the cloth and the density of the embroidery show. We make pastels that do not look thin.

  • Grey-lilac · sage · apricot · blue-grey pastels
  • Gold-thread embroidered jeogori with layered organza skirt
  • Volume petticoat included to hold the silhouette

Two women,
side by side

A mother's hanbok should never be chosen on its own. We work from the moment the two mothers appear in the same photograph, designing the colour and the difference in value between the two garments together.

  • Both mothers fitted together, colours matched
  • Aunts and wedding guests can be dressed in the same palette
  • Colour arrangement planned around the family group photograph

Notice

Before you come

By appointment only

Both mothers usually come together, which needs unhurried fitting time. Please call ahead so we can set aside the hour.

Book early in season

Weekends in the spring and autumn wedding seasons fill quickly. Contact us as soon as the wedding date is set.

Overseas clients

We are about an hour from central Seoul and close to Incheon International Airport. Consultation is in Korean — please arrange interpretation in advance, or contact us on Instagram.

Colour on screen

Hanbok fabrics take colour in ways a camera and a monitor cannot fully reproduce. Please treat these images as a guide.
